# Contributing local water knowledge

Shorewise only wants locations that people genuinely use. A promising-looking stretch of water is not enough.

## What we need

For each location, please provide:

- Location name and exact launch point
- Public map link or latitude and longitude
- Sports genuinely practised there: SUP, wing foil, windsurf, kite or surf
- Evidence: operator, club, instructor, access authority or published route
- Suitable ability levels
- Best and unsafe wind directions
- Useful tide window, if applicable
- Known currents, rips, weirs, races, mud, rocks, submerged objects or shipping
- Access, parking, fees, permits and seasonal restrictions
- Whether lifeguard or organised safety cover exists, including dates and hours
- A nearby Plan B when the location is unsuitable
- Why the Plan B is better in those conditions
- When the information was last checked
- Contributor name and relevant local experience

## Three useful local answers

The most valuable contribution is often a small, honest local decision tree:

1. Where would you send a beginner for flat water today?
2. Where would an intermediate paddler go if the main beach has offshore wind or excessive swell?
3. Where would an experienced rider go for wind, waves or surf—and what conditions make it work?

Please state when there is no sensible local option. Shorewise should never invent a recommendation merely to fill a card.

## Evidence rules

- Link to the original operator, authority, club or specialist source where possible.
- Separate confirmed facts from personal local knowledge.
- Do not publish private launch points, restricted access, personal contact details or "secret" spots without the owner/community's permission.
- A weather forecast is not proof that access or a sport is permitted.
- River and estuary recommendations need locally understood level or tide limits before they can receive a green signal.

## Corrections

Safety-critical corrections should include the location, the incorrect statement, the corrected wording, a source and the date checked.
